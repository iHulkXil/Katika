# Katika.Bet Roadmap

## Sprint sequence

1. **Sprint 1 — Foundation**  
   Responsive application shell, navigation, branded landing page, placeholder routes, and future architecture documentation.
2. **Sprint 2 — Privy authentication**  
   Add wallet-based identity and account lifecycle after the foundation is stable.
3. **Sprint 3 — Demo wallet and credit ledger**  
   Add clearly separated development/demo credits with server-owned accounting.
4. **Sprint 4 — Provably fair engine**  
   Establish seed commitments, outcome verification, and auditability.
5. **Sprint 5 — Coin Flip**  
   Add the first game flow after the fairness engine is ready.
6. **Sprint 6 — Dice**  
   Add dice gameplay and its associated history.
7. **Sprint 7 — Mines**  
   Add mines gameplay with safe session handling.
8. **Sprint 8 — Roulette**  
   Add roulette gameplay and round presentation.
9. **Sprint 9 — Rewards and missions**  
   Add reward definitions, mission progress, and claims.
10. **Sprint 10 — Referrals and leaderboard**  
    Add referral attribution and ranked views.
11. **Sprint 11 — Admin dashboard**  
    Add protected operational and audit tooling.
12. **Sprint 12 — Beta testing and polish**  
    Test the complete product flow, improve accessibility, and prepare for beta feedback.

Sprint 1 stops at the foundation. No later sprint should be started automatically.
