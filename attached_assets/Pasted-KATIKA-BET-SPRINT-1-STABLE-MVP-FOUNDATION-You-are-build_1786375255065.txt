KATIKA.BET — SPRINT 1
STABLE MVP FOUNDATION

You are building the foundation of Katika.Bet.

Katika.Bet will eventually become a Web3 gaming platform. For now, we are building a $0 MVP and will add functionality incrementally in separate sprints.

IMPORTANT:

This is Sprint 1 ONLY.

Your priority is STABILITY and SIMPLICITY.

Do not attempt to build the entire product.

Do not build casino games.

Do not build betting.

Do not build wagering.

Do not build real-money functionality.

Do not build demo credits.

Do not build the wallet system yet.

Do not build AI.

Do not use Supabase.

Do not require a paid Replit upgrade.

Do not create unnecessary microservices.

Do not create a complex monorepo.

Do not migrate the project to Next.js if Replit provides a React + Vite starter.
